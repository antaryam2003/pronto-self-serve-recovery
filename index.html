How to read this

Detected automatically badges mark every transition the system notices — the customer is never the sensor.

Auto tags on issue categories are the ones checkable against data Pronto already has (GPS pings, timestamps, task log, payment mode). Those resolve without a queue.

Human tags are the genuinely ambiguous cases — a much smaller queue than "everything".

Scope of this prototype: customer-side only. Dispatch, payments, and the Pro app are mocked with scripted data — the prototype's job is to prove the interaction model, not the infrastructure. The "Today" view is a reconstruction of the current experience assembled from public app-store reviews, the live withpronto.com site, and press coverage — it is illustrative, not a screenshot of Pronto's app. Money, names, and timings are fictional sample data.
